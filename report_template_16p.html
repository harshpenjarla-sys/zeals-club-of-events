1. Objective and Scope of SEO Conduction

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) and Inbound Content Marketing form the empirical backbone of organic user acquisition, content discoverability, and digital brand equity. This procedural report provides an in-depth, step-by-step practical demonstration of conducting an SEO audit, establishing technical indexation, deploying an inbound blog engine, and evaluating organic search discovery for the official college engagement platform zeals-club-of-events.vercel.app using Google Search Console (GSC).

Core Objectives of the SEO Conduction Procedure:

  • Search Performance Measurement: Monitor organic user search impressions, click engagement, Click-Through Rates (CTR), and SERP ranking positions across collegiate keywords.
  • Technical Indexation Audit: Verify crawl efficiency, resolve Single Page Application (SPA) rewrite conflicts (e.g., resolving the "Sitemap is HTML" error), establish robots.txt protocols, and maintain canonical tag consistency.
  • Inbound Content Marketing Engine: Engineer and publish high-ranking blog articles on topics such as AI in college events, student hackathon tactics, and cultural showcases that funnel organic searchers into registered event attendees.

Step 6: Technical SEO Auditing & Page Indexing Verification

Crawling and indexation form the absolute foundation of SEO. If Googlebot cannot index a webpage, it cannot rank in search results. The Page Indexing report is audited to identify indexing coverage and technical bottlenecks.

  • Procedural Action: Navigate to Indexing > Pages in the left sidebar to audit index coverage status.
  • Index Coverage Breakdown & Critical Error Resolution:
    • Resolution of "Sitemap is HTML": In initial deployment, Vercel's catch-all SPA rewrites redirected /sitemap.xml to /index.html. We resolved this by implementing a negative lookahead regex in vercel.json (/((?!.*\..*).*)), ensuring pure XML delivery with Content-Type: application/xml.
    • URL Inspection Status: Live URL inspection confirmed that https://zeals-club-of-events.vercel.app/ is officially indexed by Google with valid canonical tags and mobile HTTPS encryption.
